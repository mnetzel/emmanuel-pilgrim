Here, freshness is here. Some would say cold. But I prefer to save that word for when it will be minus 20... 😜

It's beautiful. I enjoy it a lot—except for the slippery side. It's really slippery. The temperature dropped very suddenly, and the weather was very wet just before. So there’s ice under the snow, which is a great recipe for finding ourselves on all fours on the floor...

Luckily, I have my faithful and strong stick. Its name is "Spine." Yes, I gave a name to my stick. But it saves me from falling at least ten times a day. I still find myself on all fours sometimes, but I get there much slower and safer with it.

But who cares—see the beauty instead. The pictures help.
