Hello everyone!

Time for Emmanuel's news. It’s been some time since the last update, and many things have happened! All good things, I can tell you straight away.

Some were life-changing for a pilgrim. I was offered a power bank that can fully recharge my phone three times and only needs one hour to charge itself. You can imagine how convenient it is for me to take advantage of any stop in a café, bar, restaurant, or church to recharge it quickly.

And now, I’ve also been offered a new tent! That is just soooo cool. I’ve realized how amazing it is to sleep in a fully waterproof environment and to have zippers to close it, keeping insects and animals outside! It’s simply fantastic. I’m not joking. It’s so nice that it made me understand how much I was struggling with the old one.

With the old tent, I had to sleep with my feet in a plastic bag to keep them dry when it rained, and I had to use an open umbrella inside the tent, positioned above my head, because it was leaking there too. The whole setup worked well enough, but I admit it wasn’t practical... The plastic bag had to be regularly pulled up over my knees, and the umbrella handle was stuck in the middle of the tent, forcing me to sleep on my side. But it didn’t rain every day, so I was just adjusting. That’s it—adjusting.

Now, with this new tent (the same model as the last one but new and upgraded), I realize how great it is not to worry about the rain outside! So, I’m all set for that and ready to keep going...

I’m leaving Slovakia and heading to Hungary. I’ve covered a lot of kilometers in Slovakia and enjoyed it immensely. The beautiful mountains, abundant water springs, and stunning forests were amazing. And, as always, I met so many kind people. Just lovely.

To summarize my journey in Slovakia for those following my path: I visited Šaštín-Stráže, Skalka (Trenčín), Butkov, Levoča, Litmanová, Ľutina, Gaboltov, Święta Góra Jawor (technically 120 meters in Poland!), and finally Klokočov.

Now, I’m heading to Esztergom on the Hungarian side of the border, 330 km from here. My route will take me alternately through Hungary and Slovakia. I might even set up my tent on the border again, just to say I’ve slept with my head in one country and my feet in another—like I did at the border between Poland and Lithuania.

Voilà, that’s it.
     It’s short, but that makes it quicker to read!

I’m sending pictures—many of them. In one, you’ll see a bear track in the snow. You’ll also see many of the wonderful people who gave me such warm welcomes along the way. Not all of them, because I don’t take pictures all the time, and if I sent every one I have, it would be too much... But just enough to show how kind-hearted people are on the pilgrim path.

I wish you all well and happy.
