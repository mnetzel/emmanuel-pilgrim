A Few Words About Me...

My name is Emmanuel. I am originally from France. Since September 26, 2020, I have been on a traditional walking pilgrimage. This means that I follow a set of rules derived from the itinerant monasticism of the Middle Ages, and among others from various traditions where this concept exists. I carry a backpack with a tent and a sleeping bag, some equipment, and travel on foot to all the Marian sites that are indicated to me along the way.

To date, I have walked more than 8,385 km, crossing France, Belgium, Luxembourg, Germany, Poland, Lithuania, and Latvia from east to west, then Lithuania and Poland again, the Czech Republic, Slovakia, Hungary, and Romania, where I am currently.

I visit monasteries, basilicas, and sanctuaries dedicated to Mary. My approach is spiritual and has a religious aspect, but I would need to explain it in more detail. I will do so on my blog. The journey is mostly internal; the quest lies in the path that teaches me, transforms me, shapes me. The journey through hills and valleys is its support. The destination serves as motivation.

From a personal standpoint, the experience is magnificent and powerful. I immerse myself in it as deeply as possible, with the uninterrupted duration of the practice helping in this. I have discovered that freedom and happiness are not found but learned.
I work on it with all my heart.

I share as best I can with the people I meet along the way, ever since I left. This is the best I have to offer. And because I perceive a thirst, a craving in those I meet, I feel it is my duty as a pilgrim to give back what I receive by taking the time to answer questions and describe my path.

For the inner journey, most of it is ineffable. When it can be spoken, it's often inaudible... so I keep it to myself, as each spiritual experience must be, to incubate internally. Each person may eventually perceive something through my writings. That would be wonderful.

I wish you well-being and happiness.
