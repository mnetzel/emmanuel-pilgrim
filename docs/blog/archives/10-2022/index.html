Slovakia

20/10/2022 0 Comments

Slovakia...

What superb mountains!
     Just below the High Tatras—a small yet stunning mountain range at altitude.

A campfire by the edge of a clear, small lake; chanterelles (Craterellus tubaeformis) and hedgehog mushrooms (Hydnum repandum) cooked simply with olive oil and a touch of garlic for dinner. A true feast. It tastes so much better this way—especially here, in this setting.

Little moments of idyllic simplicity. I think I’ll stay here tomorrow as well. The place is utterly charming, and I’ve been assured there are no bears around. Plus, there’s a spring just a little higher up, feeding the lake. Pure happiness.

Voilà, just a little hello...

I hope and wish that all is well on your side of this message.
     Wishing you many good things.

See you soon.
