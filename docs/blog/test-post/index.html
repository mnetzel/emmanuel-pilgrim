Beginning of the Pilgrimage

24/9/2020 0 Comments

Hello.

I started my pilgrimage trip 2 days ago. I'm on my way to travel about 1500 km through France, Belgium, Luxembourg, Germany, and finally Poland. This is the first step, to get warmed. The second might be much longer, I hope...

I am going slowly and stopping at every church to pray and meditate. Today I visited 4, one in each village I passed through. Beautiful places to stop, rest, and recenter a bit...

I writing to you from my small tent in a forest where I have stopped for the night. I finished my dinner, a vegetable soup cooked on my tiny stove. That really nice and comforting to eat something hot some time.

Rain is pouring. That is good because it means the mushrooms will come! 😁
What a gift it is to eat from nature, I mean to walk and find food around. Already many hazelnuts, walnuts, soon chestnuts...

That's nice.

I wish you well and happiness, and keep you in touch.
