France

16/10/2020 0 Comments

Here I am, staying in a communal pilgrims' house in Château-Porcien.
     Happiness!

First, a hot shower... Because biodegradable pre-impregnated wipes and a large bottle of water for washing are fine for 3 or 4 days, but after that, they're not enough... In short, a shower is definitely better and feels really good...

And then, washing clothes! Well, yes, that's necessary too... Thanks to 'genius without rubbing' (a French brand of hand washing liquid soap), where you still have to rub the clothes just the same. But hey, it is a good catchy name, isn't it? So yes, washing clothes is essential. Walking socks in humid or even wet weather, with no other drying than the natural 37-degree Celsius radiator, can create quite an odor. I don’t need to draw you a picture; olfactorily, it stings a bit and can make the eyes water...
     And then washing and drying the tent! The first time it will be dry since I left...

Finally, a patch on the inflatable ground mattress, which was punctured by a bramble stem ignominiously hidden in a comfortable tuft of grass when I dropped my backpack in the wilderness... So the last few nights were less comfortable than the first ones. The ground is damp, cold, and angular without the mattress, so 3 cm of compressed air is good, but it can burst...

In short, it's a very nice little pilgrims' house. I even cooked a hot meal tonight! And I'll have hot black coffee tomorrow morning!

And I got to recharge my phone and the additional battery.
    Happiness is easy, I tell you!
