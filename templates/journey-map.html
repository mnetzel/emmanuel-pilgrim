Every journal entry has a marker. Ochre marks a place identified in the text; blue marks an estimate. A named place may be one of several stops described in a post, rather than the place where it was written. No marker claims an exact campsite or wildlife encounter.

Where no place can be identified, positions are estimated along the recorded route in proportion to the time between neighbouring entries with a location reference. Regional clues are also used; those references remain blue, and their uncertainty carries into the estimates between them. Publication dates can lag behind travel, so these are approximate positions, not a daily GPS track.
